**Checklist:**
1. **Totals:** "no less than 12 separate courses, and a minimum of 7 must be Upper Division (#100-199)". The catalog has no totals, so a 12-course total and a 7-upper-division total are added.
2. **Double counting:** Courses count once by default, which is right. The page lets a 90/100/190 seminar designated as pre-1800 or Literatures in English satisfy both requirements, "they will need to take an additional elective to still have the 12 total classes". Designations vary by semester, so this isn't encoded. The seminar rule now comes before the area rules so seminars are used for the seminar requirement first.
3. **Electives:** "An elective course is any four-unit English Department course." The FAQ excludes only the 45 series and R1A/R1B. The catalog's fixed list is replaced with every fixed 4-unit ENGLISH course below 200, plus N166. ENGIN 184 and ENGLISH 84A (2 units) are dropped.
4. **Caps:** Not expressible, so logged only. At most 2 Creative Writing courses (43A/B/C/E, 141, 143A/B/C/E), and 141 can't count twice. At most 2 four-unit Summer Session courses. At most 5 courses taken elsewhere or outside the department. Up to 2 extra-departmental or study-abroad courses by petition. At most 1 UC Extension course.
5. **Missing blocks:** Nothing missing beyond the totals and the honors-seminar option.
6. **Either/or paths:** English 190 or the H195A/B Honors Seminar. The catalog had 190 only; this is now a chooseRules.
7. **Placeholders:** None in the catalog rules.
8. **Contradictions:**
   - Shakespeare: the catalog list lacked R17 and 117F; both added. 117T is excluded per the detailed section, though the summary list on the same page includes it.
   - Pre-1800: 118 added.
   - Literatures in English: 33, 135AC, 137A, 137B and 139 added.
   - Rule order: in the catalog, Literatures in English came before the Research Seminar rule and contains 190, so a single 190 would have been used up before the seminar rule saw it. Fixed by reordering.
