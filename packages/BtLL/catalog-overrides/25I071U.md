# 25I071U: Mathematics (minor)

## Checklist
1. **Totals:** "four lower-division and five upper-division courses". The rules after the changes add up to exactly that (4 LD + 4 core + 1 elective), so no separate total rule is needed.
2. **Double counting:** Within the minor, the default (each course counts once) is right. Across programs: "Only one upper-division class may overlap between your major and the Mathematics Minor." That's cross-program and can't be encoded.
3. **Electives:** The catalog required 3 courses from a fixed non-Math list. The department requires 1 elective, which can be any upper-division Math course or a non-Math course from the Applied Math electives list. Fixed.
4. **Caps:** "At least three of the five upper division courses must be completed at UC Berkeley" is a residency rule, not encoded.
5. **Missing blocks:** The lower division (Math 51, 52, 53, and 54 or 56) was missing. Added.
6. **Either/or paths:** For the elective, it's a UD Math course or an applied elective (chooseRules). Math 54 can be replaced by Math 56.
7. **Placeholders:** none.
8. **Contradictions:** The elective count was 3 in the catalog vs 1 on the department page. The non-Math elective list differs from the department's sample clusters, but the page gives no definitive list, so the catalog list is kept.

## Changes
- Added a "Lower Division" allOf block before the core: MATH 51, 52, 53, and 54/56.
- Replaced "Upper Division" (atLeast 3 from the non-Math list) with "Upper Division Elective": 1 of (any UD MATH course not used by the core | 1 from the catalog's non-Math list).

## Not encoded / open questions
- Substitutions for Math 54 depend on the student's major: Physics 89 for Physics majors, and EECS 16A + 16B for CS/EECS majors, each with a grade of at least C. They aren't encoded because GradTrak can't condition on the major, so affected students need manual review.
- Letter grades are required. A minimum 2.0 GPA is required in the lower division and in the five UD courses. There's a P/NP exception for Sp20–Sp21.
- Only one UD course may overlap with the student's major.
- At least 3 of the 5 UD courses must be taken at UC Berkeley.
- Eligibility: the student must be declared in a major other than Mathematics and declare no later than one semester before the expected graduation term.
- The definitive pre-approved list of non-Math electives isn't stated (see `notStated`).
