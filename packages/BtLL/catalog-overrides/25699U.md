**Checklist:**
1. **Totals:** "three lower-division requirements and eight upper-division political science courses", "for a total of 12 courses". The parts already force 12: 2 introductory courses, methods, history and 8 upper-division courses. No separate total is added.
2. **Double counting:** The page says upper-division courses "can satisfy the distribution and specialization requirements in the major", and distribution may use the introductory courses (PS 1-5). Distribution and Specialization are therefore `shared`. Introductory, Methods, History and the 8 upper-division courses each count a course once. History has no POLSCI courses, so nothing overlaps there.
3. **Electives:** The 8 upper-division courses are any POLSCI course 102-189 or 191, excluding 179 and any 110, and graduate courses (200+) count. The catalog had only a placeholder. It is now an explicit list built from the catalog.
4. **Caps:** At most 3 upper-division requirements may come from other 4-year institutions (with approval). At most one non-Berkeley course may count toward specialization, and none toward distribution. PS 3 must be taken at Berkeley. None of these can be expressed, so they are logged only.
5. **Missing blocks:** The distribution requirement's upper-division options were missing (the catalog's per-subfield "distribution" lists were partial, and the Empirical Theory one had only PS 3/W3). These are now the full department ranges.
6. **Either/or paths:** Methods is PS 3 *or* Data C8 + PS 88. The catalog had two required rules; it is now one chooseRules. Specialization is in *one* of five subfields. The catalog required the lower- and upper-division rules for all five (10 rules); this is now one chooseRules of five options, each the introductory course plus 2 upper-division courses.
7. **Placeholders:** "Complete at least 8 of: Any 4-unit POLSCI 100–191-level course (excluding POLSCI 110 and POLSCI 179)" became an atLeast-8 list. The department page doesn't mention 4 units, so that restriction is not applied.
8. **Contradictions:**
   - History: the PDF lists HISTORY 1, 142, 159A, 164B, 175B, 177A, 177B and C139B, which the catalog lacks; they are added. The catalog's extras (166A, 174B, PACS 125AC, MELC 173, IAS 45, POLECON C160, RELIGST C124, N/X versions) are kept, because the PDF is from 2023 and several are cross-listings of PDF courses.
   - Political Theory: the catalog's 111AC and 118B are outside "112A-118AC" and are dropped (111AC is in American Politics' range).
   - International Relations: the catalog's 122 is outside "122A-129C" and is dropped. 138F is added (listed under both Comparative and IR).
   - Empirical Theory: 132C and 135 are added. The catalog's ECON C110 / ECON C142 / PUBPOL C142 are kept, grouped with POLSCI C135 / C131A.
