## Checklist
1. **Totals:** The only total is "A minimum of 120 units is required for graduation" (a college requirement). There's no major-specific course or upper-division total, so nothing was encoded.
2. **Double counting:** None of the pages say anything about it. Outside the technical-elective areas, no course appears in two rules (checked with a script). Inside them, INDENG 130, MECENG C127, MECENG 140 and MECENG 154 each appear in two areas.
3. **Electives:** Technical electives come from fixed area lists. Free electives are unrestricted and only fill units, so they aren't encoded.
4. **Caps:** Nothing found.
5. **Missing blocks:** Nothing missing. The Fluid Mechanics area was an empty catalog placeholder, and the department lists AEROENG C166/MECENG C166 for it.
6. **Either/or paths:** Physics B "Option 1" (7B) and "Option 2" (5B+5BL) were both emitted as required, which made them unsatisfiable. They're merged into a chooseRules: 7A+7B or 5A+5B+5BL. The 17 technical-elective areas were each emitted as required (34 courses). They're now a chooseRules: 2 areas × 2 courses.
7. **Placeholders:** "Four courses are required from two different areas…" is now the Technical Electives chooseRules. The empty Fluid Mechanics placeholder is now an area sub-rule.
8. **Contradictions:**
   - MATSCI 45 and 45L were alternatives in one group, but the department requires both, so they're split.
   - The Science Elective list lacked BIOLOGY 1B and CHEM 3B/3BL, so they're added.
   - The technical-elective areas lacked MECENG C144 (Computational Tools), AEROENG 122 (Space Technologies) and the Fluid Mechanics course, so they're added.
   - Controls: the older pages also allow ELENG C128/MECENG C134, but the Class of 2027 flowchart says "Effective Fall 2026, MECENG 132 is the only option for this requirement", so the catalog's ME 132-only rule is kept.
