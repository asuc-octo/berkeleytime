## Checklist
1. **Totals:** "Complete at least 26 upper division units in the major". Added as a program-wide upper-division units total. There is no overall course count.
2. **Double counting:** extra courses in a distribution group count as electives, except lower-division Philosophy. The default order (distribution groups before electives) handles this. The page doesn't say whether one course can fill two distribution groups (see open questions).
3. **Electives:** "at least three electives found on the comprehensive CogSci course list" (or by petition). The catalog placeholder is now an `atLeast 3` rule over the catalog's 249-course list.
4. **Caps:** none stated. The distribution courses "must be taken on campus", a residency rule that isn't encoded.
5. **Missing blocks:** nothing missing. The prerequisites (stats, calculus, programming) and lower division (CogSci 1, discrete math, neuroscience) are all in the catalog rules.
6. **Either/or paths:** none. Each distribution group is one-of, already `atLeast 1`.
7. **Placeholders:** the Electives placeholder is now a real rule.
8. **Contradictions:** the department lists courses the catalog lacks (Math 10A; MCB W61/C61/C64; CogSci C102/Psych C129; Psych 122; CogSci C133/Ling C189; Psych 107, 163, 167AC; Classics 36). The catalog lists courses the department doesn't (PSYCH 164 under Cognitive Psychology, COGSCI 190 under Computational Modeling, PSYCH 166 under Society, Culture, and Cognition). The department page says the distribution courses "must be selected from the listed options", so it wins.

## Not encoded / open questions
- Letter grade in all major courses, C- or better in the prerequisites, a maximum of two attempts per prerequisite category, a 2.0 upper-division major GPA, and distribution courses taken on campus.
- AP credit (AP Stats 3+, AP Calculus 3+).
- Elective petitions ("upper division course with an explicit connection to the study of the mind"): they need a manual check, and petitioned courses don't count toward the 26 units.
- Whether one course can fill two distribution groups (CogSci 132, C126, 181 and Psych 164 appear in several): not stated, so each course counts once.
- Graduate courses on the catalog's elective list are kept unverified, because the department spreadsheet couldn't be fetched.
