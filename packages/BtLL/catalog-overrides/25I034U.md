## Checklist
1. **Totals:** The department requires "five letter-graded courses (minimum of 20 units)": 3 core plus 2 electives. The course count is encoded through the two rules. The 20-unit minimum isn't encoded (see below).
2. **Double counting:** Within the minor, courses count once (the default). Across programs, "Only one required course may be used to simultaneously satisfy both your major and minor requirements". GradTrak can't check that.
3. **Electives:** Any upper-division course with the prefixes CHINESE, EALANG, JAPAN, KOREAN, MONGOLN or TIBETAN. Encoded as a `matching` rule.
4. **Caps:** "One related course from another UC Berkeley department or an approved study abroad program may count" with approval and a substitution request. It's a petition-based exception, so it's not encoded.
5. **Missing blocks:** CHINESE 10B/10X (or placement) is a *declaration* prerequisite, listed under "Minor Declaration Requirement Coursework" and not among the five minor courses. It's not added because placement-exam equivalence can't be checked.
6. **Either/or paths:** CHINESE 7A or 7B may replace one of the three upper-division core courses. They're encoded as one shared group inside the core `atLeast` rule.
7. **Placeholders:** Both catalog placeholders (Core, Electives) were converted to real rules.
8. **Contradictions:** The catalog says Core needs "at least 2"; the department says three, and the department is followed. The catalog allows graduate 200-296 courses and 7A/7B as an elective, and excludes 195-199. The department says only "upper-division", so the rules follow that (see open questions).
