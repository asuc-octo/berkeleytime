## Checklist
1. **Totals:** "a minimum of five (5) courses" after admission. These are exactly CE 60, CE 120, CE 122/123 and two electives, so the fixed rules already enforce the total. No separate total rule was added.
2. **Double counting:** within the minor, the default (each course counts once) is right. "Only one course of the minimum five (5) courses may be counted for both a student's major and the minor" is a cross-program cap and isn't encoded.
3. **Electives:** a fixed list of 9 entries (122 or 123, 124, 132, C133/ME C180, 140, 165, 166, 175, 193). The catalog list was missing 122, 123, 132 and C133, and wrongly included 121.
4. **Caps:** only the one-course major overlap, which isn't encoded.
5. **Missing blocks:** the required "CE 122 or CE 123" course was missing from the catalog rules.
6. **Either/or paths:** Math 51+52 **or** Math 16A+16B. The catalog's rule required one course from each sequence.
7. **Placeholders:** none.
8. **Contradictions:** the catalog required CIVENG C30 and MECENG C85 as two separate courses, but they're one cross-listed course. CE 121 is on the catalog's elective list but not the department's.

## Changes
- Replaced Mathematics Options with chooseRules: (MATH 51 and 52) or (MATH 16A and 16B).
- Replaced Physics and Engineering with two groups: Physics 7A/8A, and the single cross-listed CE C30/ME C85 (with the W versions).
- Replaced Upper Division Core with Required Courses: CE 60, CE 120, and CE 122 or 123.
- Replaced Structural Electives with the department's list (2 of 122, 123, 124, 132, C133/ME C180, 140, 165, 166, 175, 193). CE 121 is dropped.

## Not encoded / open questions
- Admission requires an overall GPA of 3.0 and a 3.0 GPA in the prerequisites. The minor requires a GPA of at least 2.0 and letter grades. None of this is encoded.
- No more than one minor course may count toward the major (cross-program, not encoded).
- The five courses must be completed after admission, and the minor can't delay graduation (not checkable).
- CE 121 removal: confirm with CEE Academic Affairs whether it's still accepted. It is on the catalog and the 2013 form, but not on the current Instructions.
